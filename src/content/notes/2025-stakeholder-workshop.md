Co-organised the 2025 AI-Hab stakeholder workshop, with participants from Defra
and Mozaic Earth alongside UKCEH.

The useful surprise: almost none of the feedback was about accuracy. Shown a
model that classifies habitats about as well as an experienced ecologist, the
questions were consistently about *where it fails and how you would know*. That
turned into three concrete pieces of work — confidence displays on predictions,
clustering-based review of potentially mislabelled observations, and
model-disagreement analysis as a triage signal.

Worth recording because it inverted my assumption about what an applied model
owes its users. The headline accuracy number bought us the meeting. It was the
error structure that people actually wanted to talk about.
